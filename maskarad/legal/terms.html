1. What this service is

Masquerade is a free website that helps you make videos with AI characters. You upload your video, and the AI replaces the person in the frame with a fictional character while keeping the moves, expressions and sound. You can pick a ready-made character or have the AI draw one from a text description. Up to {{MAX_SECONDS}} seconds of video are processed; the rest is trimmed. Finished videos carry a note in their file metadata that they were made with AI.

The result is created by AI, so it may differ from what you expect. The site doesn’t guarantee that every video will be of the same quality.

2. Who pays and for what

The site doesn’t accept money. The AI runs on fal.ai and is paid from your own fal.ai account: you connect your fal.ai key, and fal.ai charges your balance at its own rates. Roughly, that’s {{VIDEO_PRICE}} per video up to {{MAX_SECONDS}} seconds and {{IMAGE_PRICE}} per custom character. Exact prices and your charge history are in your fal.ai dashboard.

Questions about charges and refunds are handled by fal.ai under its own rules. The site never receives your money and can’t refund it.

A ready-made character’s HD reference photo is prepared once and then shared with all users. If it doesn’t exist yet, it’s prepared before the first video with that character, using the key of whoever picked it, and the site warns about this in advance.

3. Your fal.ai key

Your key is stored on the site’s server in encrypted form and used only for your own videos and characters. You can remove it from the site on the Account tab and revoke it on fal.ai at any time. Don’t share your key with anyone.

You may not present the videos as footage of real events or real people, or use the videos for deception, fraud or harassment.
