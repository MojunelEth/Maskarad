How it works

Three steps, a few minutes of waiting, and your character is on screen.

  1. 01 · Film

    Film yourself

    5–10 seconds, one person in the frame, face and hands visible, even light and a phone that stands still.

  2. 02 · Cast

    Pick a character

    Take a ready-made one or describe your own: the AI draws a new face and look in about half a minute.

  3. 03 · Render

    Get your video

    Your moves, expressions and voice, played by the character. A clean file with no watermarks, ready to post.
