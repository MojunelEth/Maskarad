Guide

How to run an AI character page

Short videos with recognizable AI characters are among the most talked-about formats on social media right now. This guide covers the whole path, from the idea for a character to a series of videos and your first income. Everything you do by hand happens on the AI video tab.

Why it works

People scroll fast, and a video has less than a second to stop them. A character with a memorable look does that better than any headline, because viewers recognize them from the first frame.

When the same character repeats a signature gesture in a new everyday situation, each new video feels familiar before the joke even lands, and people start waiting for the next one.

This used to take an actor, makeup and costumes. Now a phone is enough: you act out the scene, and the AI replaces you with the character while keeping your moves, expressions and voice.

1. Create your character

Take a ready-made character or draw your own: on the AI video tab, click “Your own character” and describe the look. To see a ready-made character’s whole outfit from every side, click the arrows in the corner of their card. A good description of your own character has four parts.

PartWhat to describeExample
Age and typeWho this is at a glance“A man in his forties”, “a lively retiree”
Hair and faceWhat you can see from a distanceHair color and shape, beard, glasses
OutfitOne recognizable look for every videoA yellow raincoat over a sharp suit
Signature detailsOne or two things people rememberA huge umbrella, a gold whistle, turquoise sneakers

Example description

A man in his forties with a perfectly groomed red beard, a bright yellow raincoat over a sharp suit, round glasses and a huge walking-stick umbrella.

Then give the character a personality in one sentence: who they are and what delights or annoys them. Also invent a signature gesture, one move they repeat in every video:

  • straightening a collar;
  • lowering sunglasses;
  • blowing a whistle.

The AI transfers the whole character, clothes included. If you want to stay in your own suit, describe that suit in the character’s look.

Don’t copy real people or other creators’ famous characters. Such accounts get banned after complaints, and the site won’t draw them anyway. Your own character is also worth more, because the rights are yours.

2. Film yourself

The AI copies your moves, expressions and background and replaces only you. The cleaner the original video, the more alive the character looks.

DoAvoid
Keep one person in the frameA second person: the AI may replace the wrong one
Keep your face and hands visibleMasks, dark glasses, a cap over your eyes
Bare hands, kept inside the frameGloves and mittens, or hands at the very edge of the frame: the AI may draw extra or merged fingers
Move your hands smoothly, a bit slower than usualFast waving: the fingers turn into a blur
Face the light: a window in front or to the sideA window behind you: your face ends up in shadow
Put the phone on a shelf or lean it against somethingA handheld, shaky camera: the character “floats”
Film full length or from the waist upCropping off your head or feet
Vertical 9:16, 5–10 secondsLong or horizontal videos: we only use the first 10 seconds
Upload the original file from your phone’s cameraA video re-downloaded from TikTok or Instagram: it’s compressed, small and has their logo
Smooth moves: gestures, walking, dancingSharp spins with your back to the camera, jumps

Build each scene in three beats:

  1. Action right away. No intro: the first second decides whether people keep watching.
  2. The signature gesture in the middle.
  3. An unexpected twist at the end.

3. Make the video

  1. On the AI video tab, pick a character.
  2. Upload your clip.
  3. Tick the box confirming that the video shows only you or people who agreed to it.
  4. Click “Create video”.

Generation usually takes a few minutes. You can keep the page open, and if you close it, the video will wait for you on the Account tab.

What you get:

  • your own sound and background;
  • a clean file with no watermarks or logos;
  • a file you can download for about 24 hours.

If the AI couldn’t handle the video, reshoot the scene using the tips above and try again.

4. Build a series, not a single video

One viral video is luck, a series is a strategy. Start with ten short scenes with one character and post them over a couple of weeks.

ScenePlaceEveryday situation
1KitchenMorning coffee goes wrong
2ElevatorA stranger presses every button
3ShopThe queue that never moves
4YardThe neighbor’s car alarm, again
5CouchThe phone battery dies at 1%
  • Film in batches. Set aside one day and film all ten scenes: it’s easier to keep the look and light the same.
  • Change the place, not the character. The character and the gesture stay the same.
  • Keep an ideas list. Every everyday situation that annoys or amuses you is a ready scene.
  • Add a second character later. Characters can “answer” each other in neighboring videos, and that creates a story people follow.

5. Post

  • A separate account for the character with the same name and avatar on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts and X. In the bio, say honestly that the character is fictional and made with AI.
  • Post regularly. One video a day beats ten in one evening followed by a silent week.
  • Keep captions short: the character’s name and the point of the scene. Ready captions for our characters are below.
  • Mark the video as AI-generated when you post it. The platforms require it for realistic AI videos, and unmarked ones may be hidden or the account blocked.

Every platform also asks you to mark AI content when posting. The setting names change from time to time; this is where to look:

PlatformWhere to mark AI content
TikTokPost settings: the AI-generated content toggle
InstagramBefore sharing, under advanced settings: the AI label
YouTube ShortsWhen uploading: answer “Yes” to the altered or synthetic content question

6. Watch the right numbers

Likes are misleading. Three numbers matter more, because they answer the main question: do viewers want the next episode?

NumberWhat it tells youIf it’s low

SharesWhether the joke is worth sending to a friendMake the twist at the end stronger
Profile visits and followsWhether people want more of this characterMake the profile clearer: who the character is and what comes next

7. How to earn from it

Money comes once the character has a steady audience. These are the real options:

OptionHow it worksWhen it fits
Brand dealsA brand pays for a scene with its productBrands like characters: they don’t get tired, don’t get into scandals and always stay in character
Platform payoutsTikTok and YouTube share revenue with creatorsOnce you meet the program’s requirements; check each platform’s rules
Custom videosA local café or shop orders a series with your characterInstead of a regular ad, even with a small audience
Merch and licensingStickers, T-shirts, selling rights to the lookWhen the character is well known

An honest word about risks: most pages don’t take off with the first series, and there’s no fast, guaranteed money here. Stay away from offers to launch a “character token” or a memecoin: such schemes often turn out to deceive followers and cause trouble for the creator.
